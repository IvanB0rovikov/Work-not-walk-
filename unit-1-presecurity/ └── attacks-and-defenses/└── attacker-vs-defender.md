Taking the role of a defender and considering that malicious software is already downloaded to my laptop, I have several options for avoiding surveillance. First, I can take preemptive measures, such as installing robust antivirus software or setting a BIOS password that must be entered before the device boots up. If the malicious software is already on the laptop, however, I must rely solely on my own knowledge; since I have no information about the specific software involved, I need to make a habit of constantly checking my device for unauthorized programs.
