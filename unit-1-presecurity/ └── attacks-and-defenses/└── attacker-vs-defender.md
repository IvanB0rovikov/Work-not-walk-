Step 2 — Think like an attacker
Describe one realistic attack against your chosen system. Be specific - "someone could hack it" is not an attack, "an attacker tricks me into clicking a phishing link that captures my email password" is.

Write 3–5 sentences covering:

What the attacker would try to do
How they would do it (the technique, in plain language)
What they would gain if it worked
